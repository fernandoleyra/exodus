## 1. DATA REALITY

### 1.1 The cadence truth
**No real-time bilateral migration stock or flow dataset exists anywhere [verified].** Tiers:
- **Sub-hour**: GDELT 2.0 (15-min slots), USGS quakes (1–5 min), GDACS (15–60 min). Only these may carry "LIVE" chrome.
- **Daily**: IDMC IDU (T+0), UNHCR ODP situations (registration-gated).
- **Monthly (fastest true bilateral)**: Eurostat `migr_asyappctzm` — asylum applicants, monthly, EU+EFTA destinations only, data through **2026-08**, updated **17.09.2026**, 103,640,312 rows → ~4-week latency ceiling [verified]. Siblings: `migr_asypenctzm`, `migr_asyaccm`, `migr_asytpsm`, `migr_asytpfm`, `migr_asyumactm`. Quarterly: `migr_asydcfstq`, `migr_asytpfq`, `migr_asywitfstq`.
- **Annual**: Eurostat `migr_imm*`/`migr_emi*`/`migr_pop*`, OECD IMD, WDI, Gaskin & Abel.
- **Quinquennial/frozen**: UN DESA IMS, WB GBMD, Abel & Cohen, OECD DIOC, QuantMig.

## 2. MODELLING REALITY

**The honest baseline number.** Robinson & Dilkina (COMPASS '18, doi:10.1145/3209811.3209868) [verified from Tables 2–3]: with a production function, at global scale, gravity-exponential CPC **0.16**, gravity-power **0.16**, radiation **0.16**, extended radiation **0.16**, XGBoost+extended **0.21**, ANN+extended **0.22**. **Without** a production function: XGBoost+extended **0.43**, ANN+extended **0.40** — i.e. **roughly half the achievable accuracy lives in getting origin outflow totals right.** CPC = Bray–Curtis: `2·Σ min(Tᵢⱼ,T̂ᵢⱼ) / (ΣTᵢⱼ + ΣT̂ᵢⱼ)`. Radiation belongs in the UI as a *null model*, never the baseline.

**Estimator.** PPML (Poisson quasi-ML, clustered SEs), not OLS-on-logs: Santos Silva & Tenreyro (2006, *REStat* 88(4):641–658) [verified]. Caveat the literature is unsettled — Martin & Pham (*Applied Economics* 2020, 52(26):2766–2779) find alternatives less biased under economically-determined zeros [verified]. **Never write "PPML is unbiased" in the UI.** Tooling: `fixest::fepois`, Stata `ppmlhdfe`, Python `pyfixest`.

**Nested logit with multilateral resistance (Bertoli & Fernández-Huertas Moraga, *JDE* 102:79–100, 2013 [verified]).** `Iᵢᵣ = λᵣ·ln Σ_{j∈r} exp(lnAᵢⱼ/λᵣ)`; `M̂ᵢⱼ = Oᵢ·P(r|i)·P(j|i,r)`. Row sums equal `Oᵢ` by construction, so closing a corridor **redistributes** rather than deletes — this is the property any "close the Mediterranean" scenario requires.

**Shippable coefficients — Ortega & Peri (2013, *Migration Studies* 1(1):47–74), all [verified] from the paper:** `β_yd` **+0.76**; European-pairs override **+1.90**; non-European destination **+0.42**; `β_yo` **−0.33** (no hump under country FE: −0.31 below / −0.32 above world median); colony **+0.85** (same empire post-1945) / **+0.77** (tie ended 1945) — **these are alternative specifications of one dummy, adding both double-counts**; entry-law tightening **−0.06** non-European / **−0.02** pooled; Maastricht **+10%** intra-European. **Their estimator is `log(flow+1)` OLS with FE — exactly what §PPML forbids.** Ship as day-one placeholders, labelled. Diaspora elasticity **≈+1.0** (Beine, Docquier & Özden, *JDE* 95:30–41, 2011) [verified] — *not* 0.70. Visa requirement **−40% to −47%** inflow, **+2.8% to +16.9%** diversion (Ramos, IZA WoL, Feb 2016) [verified] — but this is a reduced-form gravity effect and **cannot be dropped raw into a λ-scaled nested logit**; calibrate so the *simulated* drop lands at 40–47%. `β_dist`, `β_comlang`, `β_contig`: signs verified, **magnitudes UNVERIFIED — do not attribute −0.80 to any paper.**

**CEPII Gravity 202211 (1948–2020, Etalab 2.0) field names [verified]:** `dist`, `distcap`, **`distw_harmonic`**, `distw_arithmetic`, `contig`, `comlang_off`, `comlang_ethno` (9% threshold), `comcol`, `col45`, `comrelig`, **`scaled_sci_2021`** (Facebook SCI — a strong network proxy). **`distw`, `distwces`, `colony`, `smctry` DO NOT EXIST in Gravity — legacy GeoDist only, unmaintained.**

**Migration hump: contested.** Clemens (IZA DP 13614, 2020): rise slows ≈$5,000, reverses ≈**$10,000** PPP; within-country elasticity +0.35 all destinations, +0.74 to rich [verified]. **PPP base year UNVERIFIED.** Benček & Schneiderheinze (*World Development* 182, 2024) find the hump disappears under country FE with a significant **negative** relation [verified]. Ship as a toggle. The functional form `h(y)=1−((ln y−ln y*)/σ_y)²` is **this project's own construction, [UNVERIFIED] as literature**; with y*=8000/σ=2.2 it peaks at 1 and crosses zero at ≈$900 and ≈$71,000 (arithmetic verified) — but **y* must be rebased: WDI `NY.GDP.PCAP.PP.KD` is constant 2021 international $, not 2011 PPP.**

**Cohort-component projection (WPP 2024, 237 countries, single-year ages, 1950–2100; 2026 revision postponed to 2027 [verified]).** Unit normalisation is the difference between a working engine and nonsense: **`SRB`/100** (ships as 105.8 males per 100 females — using it raw gives a 99.1% male birth share), **`ASFR`/1000**, **population×1000** [verified]. `S[a]=L[a+1]/L[a]`; births `B=Σ_{15..49} f[a]·0.5·(P_F[a][t]+P_F[a][t+1])`. **The .csv.gz files are multi-member gzip — a single-member reader returns 634 bytes (header, zero rows) with NO ERROR** [verified]. Filter `LocTypeName='Country/Area'`. **VarID 2 (Medium) − VarID 7 (Zero migration) is the officially-sanctioned migration counterfactual and the product's core primitive.** Bisection for "migration needed to hold OADR constant" needs **~14–17 iterations at 1e-4 relative tolerance, not 40**, and **monotonicity fails for profiles weighted toward 65+ — check bracket endpoints straddle the target first.**

**Absorption / capacity.** Liebig minimum, **over per-capita STOCK RATIOS ONLY**: `AnnualIntakeCapacity = min_{k∈K_stock}[(x_k/target_k − 1)·pop] / horizon`. Demonstrated live: taking the min across all 12 indicators yields ≤0 for **every country on Earth** — 216/216 sit at or below a 100% electricity target, 214/214 at or below 100% water [verified]. `K_stock` = physicians, nurses/midwives, beds, health spend per capita, dwellings. **WHO SDG threshold 4.45 doctors+nurses+midwives per 1,000 is a FLOOR, explicitly not an optimum [verified].** The formula holds service stock fixed while growing population — migrants bring physicians, so it **systematically understates**; say so.

**UN Replacement Migration (ESA/P/WP.160, 21 Mar 2000), Table 8, 1995–2050, [verified from the primary text]:** Scenario V (PSR floor 3.0) — EU 153.6M, Japan 94.8M, Germany 40.5M, USA 44.9M, Italy 35.1M. **Scenario VI (hold 1995 PSR) — EU 700.5M (~12.7M/yr), Japan 553.5M, Republic of Korea 5.149 BILLION, Italy 119.7M, Europe 1.386bn**, with the table's own footnote **"Scenario VI is considered to be unrealistic."** Scenario III (constant population) — EU 47.5M, Italy 12.9M. **UN PSR = P_15–64/P_65+** — a `wap` defined as 20–64 systematically overstates replacement need. Display Sanderson–Scherbov POADR alongside (Germany +11.3% vs conventional +49.2% [verified]).

**Composite indices — all weights are author constructions, no literature behind any number.** DPI weights `[0.20,0.25,0.30,0.15,0.10]` sum to 1.00 (verified). Three defects: cross-sectional Z makes DPI incapable of showing *global* escalation; the GDACS term should use the first-class `alertscore`; the UCDP term cannot be weekly (GED final is annual, Candidate monthly with ~1-month lag and coverage only from **2011**). **DPI inherits the worst cadence among its terms → monthly-to-quarterly → DPI can never sit under LIVE chrome.** Robust Z must be `(x−median)/max(1.4826·MAD, ε)` with ε = one meaningful unit, and suppressed entirely for units with insufficient nonzero history — **MAD is 0 for most admin units and the naive form emits Infinity/NaN.** DFS `exp(−Δt/τ)` with τ = one cadence period scores a perfectly on-time value at 0.37; **τ_GDELT=0.25 h renders the freshest feed in the stack as stale within 30 minutes.** Use τ ≈ 3–4× cadence or score against expected-next-update.

**Matching function**: `M = A·U^α·V^(1−α)`, **α ≈ 0.6** (Petrongolo & Pissarides, *JEL* 39(2):390–431, range 0.5–0.7) [verified], not 0.5. **JVR = V/(V+O)×100** (Eurostat definition).

## 6. ETHICAL HARD RULES

1. **Operational Latency Floor (project policy, ours, not inherited):** movement layers at **≥7-day lag, ≥monthly bucket, ADM1 or coarser wherever origin is disaggregated**; ADM2 destination only with origin fully undisaggregated at ≥30-day lag and ≥quarterly. Enforced server-side in `layer_policy.yaml`. **Capacity is live and fine-grained; people are not.**
2. **No individual-level data, ever.** CI fails on any schema containing `person`, `individual`, `case_id`, `applicant`, `biometric`, `name`, `dob`. Minimum aggregation admin1, n≥100.
3. **Suppression:** K=25 (ADM0×ADM0), K=100 (ADM1/ADM2), denominator floor 10,000 population, **complementary suppression** (suppress the next-smallest cell in any margin with exactly one suppression), **frozen suppression** (once suppressed, always suppressed — published append-only ledger). Rounding `5·round(n/5)` <1000, `10·round(n/10)` <10k, 3 s.f. above — **rounding is not a safeguard on its own; suppression runs first.** Gridded people layers H3 ≤ res 3. **HDX rule adopted: global re-identification risk <3% and no record violating 3-anonymity** [verified as HDX's sharing threshold; the 5% figure was a 2019 case-study outcome].
4. **No route geometry.** Corridors are great-circle arcs between ADM0 centroids. Reject any movement-layer `LineString` with >2 vertices. No waypoints, crossing points, bathymetry, vessel or aircraft telemetry.
5. **No cross-tab of nationality with geography finer than ADM0.** Ethnicity, religion, sexual orientation, health, political opinion are **not layers**. Reject at the query planner with **HTTP 403** and a machine-readable policy body (**not 451** — RFC 7725 reserves that for legally-compelled blocking).
6. **EU AI Act:** never implement any Annex III(7) function — (a) polygraph-like; (b) assessing a risk, including security/irregular-migration/health risk, posed by a person entering; (c) assisting examination of asylum/visa/residence applications; (d) detecting/recognising/identifying natural persons. Nor Art. 5(1)(g) biometric categorisation. **Dates, now citable: Art. 5 applicable 2 Feb 2025; new Art. 5(1)(ba)/(bb) from 2 Dec 2026; Annex III high-risk from 2 Dec 2027 and Annex I from 2 Aug 2028, per Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (Digital Omnibus on AI), OJ 24 July 2026** [verified]. Also: ECHR Protocol 4 Art. 4 / Charter Art. 19(1) bar any group-level removal-candidate or cohort-scoring feature.
7. **No scalar capacity.** Banned output strings: `capacity limit`, `carrying capacity`, `maximum`, `threshold`, `saturation`. Required label: "Modelled labour-market absorption under assumption set «name» — not a policy limit."
8. **No departure forecasting, no outflow-risk score, no origin "pressure index".** Forecast targets are restricted to arrivals-and-needs at destination.
9. **Denominator mandate:** no absolute people-count renders without a simultaneous per-capita value. Default encoding is per-capita. Symmetric in/out arcs by default.
10. **Lexicon lint (CI, exit non-zero):** `illegal` (of a person), `alien`, `flood`, `wave`, `surge`, `influx`, `tide`, `swarm`, `invasion`, `bogus`, `burden`, `host` (→ destination), `carrying capacity`. Structure the rule on the **seven dehumanising source concepts** from Mendelsohn & Budak (arXiv:2502.13246, ACL 2025): animal, vermin, parasite, physical pressure, water, commodity, war [verified]. **UNGA Res. 3449 (XXX), 9 Dec 1975 requires "non-documented or irregular migrant workers"** [verified] — this is a General Assembly basis, not style preference.
11. **Human decision required.** GDPR Art. 22 bars decisions based *solely* on automated processing. Solver returns a ranked shortlist of 3 with per-term score decomposition and the runner-up's losing term; **no code path from `solve()` to `commitPlacement()` without a human actor id.** Override rate published.
12. **Protected attributes excluded from `E_ij` training.** CI gate: adversary AUC ≤0.65 predicting each protected attribute from score+features (a project convention, documented as chosen). Answers Alajak, Burnazoglu, Leurs & van Schie, *Social Inclusion* 14 (2026) art. 10923, doi:10.17645/si.10923 [verified], which found the Dutch GeoMatch deployment "prioritises aggregate optimisation over individual opportunities" with disproportionate discrimination risk on ethnicity, gender and marital status.
13. **Banned visual motifs:** crosshairs, reticles, radar sweeps, threat pulses over populated places, dossier/mugshot cards, person silhouettes, TARGET/TRACK/LOCK/WATCHLIST in code or copy. Rename at schema level: `watchlist→saved_views`, `target→area_of_interest`, `entity→place|indicator`. Global search indexes **no person-shaped type**.
14. **Viewer privacy:** zero third-party scripts, cookieless self-hosted analytics, 7-day retention, IP truncation /24 IPv4 and /48-or-shorter IPv6 (**/48 is a ceiling, not a target — it can still single out a household; prefer /32**).
15. **Simulation and operations physically separated** (schema, auth scope, colour chrome, non-dismissible "SCENARIO — MODELLED, NOT POLICY" watermark). Public aggregates suppress cells k<10. Opaque pseudonyms from the API. Density fields, never trajectories.
16. **Redress with an SLA** (Signal Code's Right to Rectification and Redress, Jan 2017 [verified]): `.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/harm-report.yml`, monitored address, **30-day response**, power to de-publish a layer, public de-publication log.
17. **Default camera leads with the Global South.** Justification: UN DESA IMS 2024 — **53% of all international migrants moved within their own region of origin** (83% Eastern/South-Eastern Asia, 73% LAC, 63% Central/Southern Asia) [verified]. Equal Earth for any 2D fallback, never Web Mercator for people-density.
18. **Colour of people is never red/orange/yellow.** Warm hues are reserved for *service and rights deficits* — red marks a failure of provision, never a quantity of persons.

**The five-part thesis.**
1. **Nobody couples capacity to allocation.** Every incumbent answers "how many are moving?" or "how many will move?" None answers *"how many can X absorb, into which occupations and which subnational units, and what is the welfare-maximising assignment across destinations?"* That is an optimisation problem and no open tool solves it.
2. **Nobody makes the model editable by the policymaker.** Atlas and Foresight are read-only outputs of someone else's assumptions. Assumption sliders with visible provenance + a signed, forkable, diffable scenario JSON is the wedge.

## 8. THE 10 SHARPEST CONSTRAINTS ON THE MVP

1. **Licensing is the largest unmanaged risk, and it blocks ingest, not launch.** UN DESA IMS (the bilateral spine) and ReliefWeb are non-commercial, no-derivatives; ACLED, EM-DAT, GADM, MIPEX and IDMC are each blocked on independent grounds; **UNHCR's terms are contradictory across two verification passes and must be resolved in writing**; gbOpen is ~42% ODbL at ADM1. `license_id` + `redistributable` on every row, gated at export, or the project cannot ship a CC BY bundle at all.
2. **The offline snapshot is architecture, not convenience.** migrationdataportal.org, frontex.europa.eu, dtm.iom.int and cbp.gov all 403 scripted clients; HAPI and DTM need identifiers; GDELT DOC 429s on first burst. `npm run dev` with the network off must render 200+ countries, working sliders and a working solver.
3. **"Live" is one thin monthly layer, and everything else must say so.** The only sub-annual bilateral series are six Eurostat monthly asylum tables (EU+EFTA destinations, ~4-week lag, through 2026-08) and UNHCR `asylum-applications`. Brand these "Live signal"; everything else "Reference" with `vintage_date` and `latency_days` badges. **Never write "real-time" for stocks or flows.**
4. **Occupational resolution collapses.** ILOSTAT gives ISCO **1-digit** crossed with birth status and 2-digit *without* it; Eurostat LFS/SES are 1-digit (`lfsa_egised` has 12 `isco08` members); **JOLTS has no occupational dimension at all**; only `jvs_a_isco3_r1` (experimental, OJA-derived, NUTS-1, 2019–2024) reaches 3-digit. **Employment × 4-digit ISCO × foreign-born does not exist in any free API.** Build the schema at 4-digit, populate at 1–2, and say so in the UI.
5. **Vacancy data covers ~40 countries, not 200** — Eurostat `jvs_q_isco_r2` carries 37 national reporters [verified]. **Countries with no vacancy data render "no demand data", never zero, never interpolated.** Same for shortage lists: MIPEX caps at 56, IMPIC 33, DEMIG-QuantMig 31 — any global policy-restrictiveness layer beyond ~56 is fabrication.
6. **`estimate_kind` is non-negotiable and must bind to a visual channel.** UN DESA extrapolated **173 of 233** countries; KNOMAD BRE, Abel & Cohen, Gaskin & Abel and QuantMig are all model output; even Eurostat DE←SY carries `OBS_FLAG='e'`. Rendering these identically is the product's single largest integrity failure.
7. **Three formula defects must be fixed before coding:** the per-occupation annualisation multiplier `365/TTF_o` belongs *inside* the Σ; Module C mixes per-1,000 capacity ratios with absolute population (a factor-of-1,000 error); the soft-min needs `1/J` normalisation (six tied modules produce a spurious 27% haircut). Plus: the MILP's `z_jd` is undefined, C8 uses an absolute value, and the IPF loop oscillates once any destination ceiling binds.
8. **Most parameters are placeholders and must be badged as such.** `theta_h`, `theta_conflict`, `emigBase`, `yStar`, every `lambda`, every J-curve `E_∞/λ/τ_0`, every public-service utilisation ratio, `ε_D`, `β_vac`, `μ`, `Δs_max`, the SII weights, the DPI weights, all four acceptance coefficients. **The `labour_absorption_gap` example is negative for essentially every country under normal `u*` and uses two undefined terms — it fails its own two-maintainer gate and must not ship.** Never describe any of these as "standard" or "from the literature."
9. **Globe rendering has three gating unknowns to smoke-test in week one:** `_GlobeView` is experimental with documented GLOBE↔FLAT switching issues; `@geoarrow/deck.gl-geoarrow@0.4.2` was built against deck.gl 9.2.1 and ships no GeoJson/Icon layer; and `TerrainLayer`-on-globe has two contradicting official pages. Global H3 ceiling is **r4 (288,122 cells)**, `highPrecision` stays `'auto'`, arcs need per-layer `cullMode:'none'` plus a mandatory opaque sphere, and every millisecond figure in every pack is an unmeasured budget.
10. **Build strictly in this order, each running end-to-end from a cold install before the next begins:** globe + country choropleth from snapshot → provenance drawer → headroom calculator → corridor arcs → allocation solver → scenario URL → live refresh → incident layer. Everything else goes to `BACKLOG.md`. **Cut from v1:** websockets, global admin2, agent-based/gravity simulation, accounts/RBAC, ACLED, remittance/trade/climate layers, 3D extruded columns, and any LLM that states figures rather than merely navigating.
