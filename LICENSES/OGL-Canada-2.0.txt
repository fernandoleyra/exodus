Open Government Licence – Canada, Version 2.0
=============================================

IRCC publishes its monthly Permanent Residents and Asylum Claimants tables under this licence.
The binding is dataset-scoped and machine-readable rather than taken from a website terms page:
the open.canada.ca CKAN records f7e5498e-0ad8-4417-85c9-9b8aff9b9eda ("Permanent Residents –
Monthly IRCC Updates") and b6cbcf4d-f763-4924-a2fb-8cc4a06e3de4 ("Asylum Claimants – Monthly
IRCC Updates") both declare license_id ca-ogl-lgo. Read 2026-09-20.

The grant, verbatim from https://open.canada.ca/en/open-government-licence-canada, read
2026-09-20:

  "The Information Provider grants you a worldwide, royalty-free, perpetual, non-exclusive
  licence to use the Information, including for commercial purposes, subject to the terms
  below."

  "You are free to: Copy, modify, publish, translate, adapt, distribute or otherwise use the
  Information in any medium, mode or format for any lawful purpose."

The condition, where the provider specifies no wording of its own:

  "Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence – Canada."

That sentence appears on /#/sources.

The exclusions, which do not bite here
--------------------------------------

The licence does not grant rights in personal information, in third-party information the
provider is not authorised to license, or in names, crests, logos and other official marks. The
IRCC tables are aggregate counts rounded by the publisher to the nearest five, with small cells
suppressed, so none of these apply.

How Exodus satisfies the conditions
-----------------------------------

Attribution in the required form is on /#/sources, together with what was changed: the trailing
"Q3 Total" column dropped because it equals July alone, suppressed cells kept distinct from
published zeros, and the publisher's own rounding left as it is. The workbook stores every cell
including the numerics as an inline string with no shared string table, and the asylum file is
tab-separated despite its .csv extension; both are parsing facts rather than modifications, and
are recorded in the adapter.
