Data Licence Germany – Attribution – Version 2.0
================================================

Destatis publishes under this licence. The variant matters and is not inferred: the Destatis
Open Data page says "The statistical data can be reused in compliance with the Data Licence
Germany 2.0 conditions", and on both the English and German pages that licence name is an
anchor whose href is https://www.govdata.de/dl-de/by-2-0 — the Namensnennung (attribution)
variant, not Zero. Read 2026-09-20.

The operative grant, verbatim from https://www.govdata.de/dl-de/by-2-0, read 2026-09-20:

  "The data and meta-data provided may, for commercial and non-commercial use, in particular

    - be copied, printed, presented, altered, processed and transmitted to third parties;
    - be merged with own data and with the data of others and be combined to form new and
      independent datasets;
    - be integrated in internal and external business processes, products and applications in
      public and non-public electronic networks."

The condition:

  "Any applicable attribution by the provider has to be indicated. Changes, editing, new
  designs or other amendments must be marked as such in the source note."

Why this file exists rather than an SPDX identifier
---------------------------------------------------

DL-DE-BY-2.0 is not in the SPDX licence list, so REUSE needs it as a LicenseRef. It is a real
public licence with a canonical text at the URL above, not a bespoke grant.

A narrower grant appears inside the workbook itself
---------------------------------------------------

The Impressum of the Statistischer Bericht workbook grants only "Vervielfältigung und
Verbreitung, auch auszugsweise, mit Quellenangabe gestattet" — reproduction and distribution,
in whole or in part, with attribution. That is narrower than DL-DE-BY-2.0 and says nothing
about commercial use or derivative works. Exodus relies on the Open Data page's grant, which is
the publisher's own general statement about its statistical data and is the broader of the two;
the narrower workbook note is recorded here so the difference is visible rather than glossed.

How Exodus satisfies the conditions
-----------------------------------

Attribution is carried on /#/sources with the publisher, the workbook and the sheet. The
modifications are set out in the same entry: partner countries selected by an explicit
allow-list rather than by excluding aggregate names, the three sex categories summed, and
partner labels joined to ISO-3166-1 alpha-3.
